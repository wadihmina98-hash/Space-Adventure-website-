Space Adventure

An intense space shooting challenge. Can you successfully intercept and destroy 20 enemy starships to secure ultimate victory?

💻 Desktop Ready 📱 Mobile Optimized

💻 Desktop Controls

Fly through space using the Arrow Keys on your keyboard for complete directional movement. Tap the Spacebar to discharge your laser weapons system.

📱 Mobile Layout

Utilize the left-side X Button to fire primary weapons. Use the right-side tactical control layout map (Y to move up, A to move down, X to move left, B to move right) to pilot your craft.
