How to Play

📱 Mobile Controls (Touch):

  • Left Side Screen: Tap the blue button X to shoot out lasers.
  • Right Side Screen: Use directional buttons to fly:
  • Fly Up: Y | Fly Down: A
  • Fly Left: X | Fly Right: B

💻 PC Controls (Keyboard):

  • Attack: Press the Spacebar key to fire your lasers.
  • Movement: Use the standard Arrow Keys to steer your starship.

Game Features

  • Infinite Scaling: The game increases in velocity and intensity the longer you survive.
  • Dynamic Obstacles: Navigate through randomized fast-moving orbital asteroid configurations.
  • Mobile Optimization: Fully playable using direct touch layout inputs on tablet platforms.
